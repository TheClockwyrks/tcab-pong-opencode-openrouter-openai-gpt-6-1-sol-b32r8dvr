# A game of angles

Two paddles. One ball. A court that never quite stays the same. Carom is a quick,
precise duel where a clean return can become a curved shot, and the next rebound
can change everything. Face the machine in Solo or challenge a friend across one
keyboard in local Versus.

The opening serve gives you a moment to find your position. Then the rally starts:
meet the ball, choose your angle, and move as you strike. A rising paddle bends
the flight one way; a falling paddle bends it the other. The pale trail lets you
read the curve while every paddle hit adds a little more speed.

Two amber obstacles sway and turn through the middle of the court. Their tilted
faces offer a bank shot when a straight return feels too predictable. Catch the
right face and send your opponent chasing; misread it and be ready to recover.

Rallies build into a contest of timing, placement, and nerve. First to 11 wins,
but you need a two-point lead, so a close match stays alive beyond match point.
Take a breath between serves, watch the obstacles, and find your next angle.
